Security & Validation Architecture

  • Live Event Listeners: Uses input, change, blur, and submit events to validate every field immediately.
  • Exact Age Calculation: Parses the birth year, month, and day to verify the user is at least 13 years old.
  • Zero Plain-Text Passwords: Passwords are validated in memory and strictly excluded from localStorage.
